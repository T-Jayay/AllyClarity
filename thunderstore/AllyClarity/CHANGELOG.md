# Changelog

## 1.0.0

- Initial release.
- Allied summons (drones, turrets, Empathy Cores, Col. Droneman, ...) get an outline in your color if they are yours and in your teammates' color if they aren't.
- Pinging an ally shows it as an ally instead of an enemy: no combat icon, the outline and ping text in the ally's color, and a chat line "pinged an ally" instead of "wants to attack".
- An ally's Genesis Loop (the charging sphere, the area it will hit and the explosion) is shown in your color instead of the Wandering Vagrant's blue, and without the Vagrant's cyan screen tint, so it isn't mistaken for an enemy's. Enemies' Genesis Loops and the Wandering Vagrant stay blue.
- Client-side: nobody else needs the mod.
- In-game settings with Risk of Options (optional). Needs HookGenPatcher (mod managers install it automatically).
